Team: Arya Garg, Haley Potter, Miami Celentana. Different crew from the Gyrus/Clay team.
Lineage (IMPORTANT): Friction is an evolution of Clay — the browser Arya's other team rebranded to Gyrus for WeaveHacks (they rebranded in case W&B took issue with the name Clay; Gyrus and Clay are the same project). Clay asked how a browser protects thinking; Friction asks the same question at the OS and hardware level. Link https://github.com/Aryagarg23/Gyrus and https://aryagarg23.com/writing/gyrus when mentioning lineage.
What (from the deck "Friction — well-being in focus"): speculative interaction design for protecting flow state without inducing metric anxiety. Pain points: 68% of working adults lack focused time, 37% work consistent overtime; focus apps punish broken focus and drive burnout. Friction is an active participant in managing cognitive load: you give it a focus window (capped at 5 hours, attention psychology) and tasks (draggable from email/notes/Slack); a song-design step doubles as a micro-focusing exercise; during work it fades away completely — no timers, no reminders. When sensors read fatigue it INCREASES friction: keystrokes physically get heavier (magnetic actuation), wrist rest warms, signaling a break before burnout. When focus drifts it DECREASES friction: refocus exercises (breathing, yoga), task switch prompts. Weekly recap uses depth-of-field metaphors (sharp = flow, gaussian blur = fatigue) instead of anxiety metrics; trend data stays local on the keyboard; the brain sensor talks to the keyboard, never the app; mechanical kill switch returns everything to a dumb desk.
Hardware concept: SNN (spiking neural network) chip in the keyboard; SPEM eye tracking from bezel sensors; RF/WiFi volumetric mesh for posture without cameras; kinetic + acoustic calibration (40Hz binaural); Peltier keycaps; "Living Bits" cortisol patches in the wrist rest; three-phase calibration flow; set-theory trigger canvas (drag "high SPEM stutters" ∩ "slumped posture" → "budget friction").
Repo contents: a Figma Make export — simulated desktop OS in React (DesktopOS, FrictionKeyboard, FlowPill, DigitalMoss, ProgressiveVignette, EdgeBump, LowFocusPivot, PeekCard + fake apps). It is a design prototype, not a working OS; say so.
